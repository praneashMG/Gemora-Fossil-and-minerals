How We Build
Narratives.

Locality Matching

We source specimens from the same vein or pocket to show the diverse habits of a single geological event.

Scientific Documentation

Each collection includes a 30-page archival ledger detailing chemical composition and mine history.

Aesthetic Balancing

Collections are curated for visual balance, ensuring sizes, colors, and textures complement one another in a display.

University Donations

We facilitate the ethical acquisition and donation of collections to geological departments worldwide, providing full tax-valuation paperwork and legal provenance.

Corporate Curations

For luxury hotels and corporate headquarters, we assemble collections designed for architectural integration and high-impact lobby displays.

Selling a Private Collection?

Gemora provides professional valuation and acquisition services for established private collections. We ensure your legacy is preserved or transferred to the right hands with complete discretion.

Global travel for high-value estate valuations is available upon request.
