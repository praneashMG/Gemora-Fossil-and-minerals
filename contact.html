Acquisition Protocols

Commonly asked questions regarding our handling and sourcing processes.

Do you provide international shipping insurance?
Yes. All high-value specimens are shipped via specialized white-glove carriers and are 100% insured for their appraisal value from the moment they leave our vault until they reach your collection.
Can I visit the gallery in person?
Our principal gallery in Zurich is available for private viewing by appointment only. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance to schedule a consultation with our chief gemologist.
How do you verify the legality of fossil exports?
We strictly adhere to the 1970 UNESCO Convention on the Means of Prohibiting and Preventing the Illicit Import, Export and Transfer of Ownership of Cultural Property. Every fossil is accompanied by legal provenance paperwork.
